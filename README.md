# TrackStack

TrackStack is a flexible productivity and management platform designed to help people organize, track, and manage the things that matter to them.

It can be adapted for different needs, such as tasks, projects, progress, time tracking, files, forms, and other workflows.

The goal of TrackStack is to provide a simple and accessible tool that can grow with its users and support different kinds of personal, academic, and professional work.

Built with React, Laravel, and MySQL.
